# Warm-up: translating player language, not just words

## 1. 策划是懂玩家的，懂得怎么悄悄改概率

- **Literal:** “The designers really understand players; they know how to quietly change the probabilities.”
- **Actual meaning:** This is sarcasm, not praise. The player is accusing the team of secretly lowering gacha rates while avoiding disclosure. “策划” means the game-design/live-ops team here, and “暗改/悄悄改概率” attacks the studio's honesty, not merely the speaker's bad luck.
- **Feeling:** Angry, suspicious and betrayed. The polished sarcastic opening makes the accusation sharper.

## 2. 这波属于是逼氪了，懂的都懂

- **Literal:** “This round counts as forcing people to spend; those who understand, understand.”
- **Actual meaning:** The new event, system or offer has crossed the speaker's line from optional monetisation into coercive spending. “这波属于是…” is conversational internet phrasing; “懂的都懂” invites other experienced players to recognise the pattern without spelling out every detail.
- **Feeling:** Resigned and cynical, with an in-group nudge to players who have seen the same monetisation tactic before.

## 3. 艾琳=铁锅，新人别懵

- **Literal:** “Aileen = iron pot; newcomers, don't be confused.”
- **Actual meaning:** “铁锅” is the community's new nickname for Aileen, prompted by her large round shield looking like a wok/pot. The sentence is onboarding new players into an emerging in-joke; it does not mean a second item or character.
- **Feeling:** Playful, helpful and slightly proud of being an insider.

## 4. 白嫖党表示毫无压力 [doge]

- **Literal:** “The free-to-play crowd says there is absolutely no pressure [doge].”
- **Actual meaning:** “白嫖党” means players who spend nothing. The doge marker usually flips the surface statement into irony: the speaker is very likely feeling the pressure but jokingly claims the opposite. Reading it as sincere praise would reverse the sentiment.
- **Feeling:** Self-mocking bravado covering frustration or anxiety about falling behind paying players.

## 5. 非酋报道，160抽一个金都没有 绷不住了

- **Literal:** “An unlucky chief reporting in: not one gold in 160 pulls; I can't hold it together.”
- **Actual meaning:** “非酋” is a self-deprecating label for someone cursed by RNG, the opposite of “欧皇”. “金” is the top-rarity result, and “绷不住了” means the speaker has reached the point where the situation is too absurd or painful to keep composure.
- **Feeling:** Disbelief and real frustration expressed through gamer self-mockery.
